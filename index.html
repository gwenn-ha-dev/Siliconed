Siliconed

enhanced by silicone

Local text-to-image generation and AI image editing for Apple silicon Macs. Z-Image Turbo and Qwen-Image-2.1, native in Swift and Metal: no Python, no server, no cloud.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Free and open source (Apache 2.0) · macOS 15 or later · M1 and later · signed and notarized

Why Siliconed

Checked against the original

Every stage is compared channel by channel with diffusers and transformers in fp32. What cannot be checked against the reference does not ship.

Full precision, still fast

Z-Image Turbo at 1024² in about 82 s on an M1 Pro, in fp32.

33 GB of model, 16 GB of RAM, zero swap

Weights stream from disk as needed. Memory and swap are measured at the worst case: 0 bytes swapped.

Private by construction

Nothing leaves your Mac, and it works offline once a model is installed.

Your models

Install full size, in 8 bits or in 4 to 6 bits; import LoRAs from Civitai or Hugging Face. Licenses and disk space are shown before the first byte.

Built to be driven by an AI

silicontrol drives the running app from a terminal with JSON output; silicontrol help is all an agent needs.

Edit with a sentence

Give Qwen-Image-2.1 one to three images and an instruction — “make it a watercolor painting”, “put the dog of image 2 next to her”. Its encoder sees the images. Drag the curtain across the result and its original to see exactly what changed.

Explore before you commit

A grid across seeds, steps, LoRA strengths, models or prompt variants, drawn in the canvas with its axes as labels. The cell you pick becomes the finished image, with the same seed.

Install what fits your Mac

Standard, Compact or Light: the publisher's weights, 8-bit versions, or a 4-bit transformer. Every file is pinned to a revision and checked by sha256, and downloads resume where they stopped.
